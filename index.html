02 · 15 min · Know the brand

OYSHO: more than just clothing

OYSHO is a Spanish fashion brand specialising in sport and leisure wear. It belongs to the Inditex Group, one of the world’s major fashion retail groups.

The brand was launched in 2001. At the beginning, OYSHO focused mainly on lingerie, underwear, swimwear, nightwear and accessories. Over time, it expanded into sportswear and activewear. Today, OYSHO describes itself as a sports and leisure brand. It creates clothing for people who enjoy movement, sport and an active lifestyle.

Five important ideas: Performance (supports physical activity) · Functionality (practical and comfortable) · Quality (materials and construction) · Innovation (new fabrics and technologies) · Design (modern, attractive styles). OYSHO Community connects people with sports activities, fitness events and other experiences.

What to remember

  • Country: Spain
  • Founded: 2001
  • Parent company: Inditex
  • Main identity: sportswear and activewear
  • Customers: people with active lifestyles
  • Values: performance, functionality, quality, innovation, design
  • Community: sports and fitness experiences

The most important interview questions

Answer formulaAnswer + Reason + Example. Give a direct answer, explain why, then add a real example if possible.

My strengths are communication, patience and responsibility. I can listen to people and understand their needs. I am also organised and punctual.

I sometimes feel nervous when I do something new. However, I try to prepare well and learn from my experience.

Question 6: Do you have any experience in sales?

I don’t have professional sales experience yet, but I am motivated to learn. I enjoy communicating with people, and I believe I can develop the necessary skills quickly.

Question 7: Can you work in a team?

Yes, I enjoy working in a team. I think communication and helping each other are very important. I am also ready to listen to other people’s ideas.

05 · 15 min · How to sell

The 6-step customer service method

1Greet the customer
2Discover their needs
3Recommend a product
4Offer alternatives
5Offer a fitting
6Finish the conversation
  1. Greet the customer — “Hello! Welcome to OYSHO. How can I help you?”
  2. Discover their needs — “What are you looking for today?” “Are you looking for something for yoga or running?”
  3. Recommend a product — “I would recommend these leggings. They look comfortable and are suitable for training.”
  4. Offer alternatives — “We also have this model in another colour.”
  5. Offer a fitting — “Would you like to try them on?”
  6. Finish the conversation — “Is there anything else I can help you with? Thank you for visiting!”

Exercise — Build a conversation

The customer wants black leggings for yoga. Use all six steps to create a conversation.

  • What kind of activity do you need them for?
  • Do you prefer a tight or loose fit?
  • We have several options.
  • This model is very popular.
  • Would you like to try another size?
  • How do they feel?

04 · 15 min · Interview language

How to answer difficult questions

How do you deal with stress?

I try to stay calm and organised. I focus on the most important tasks and solve problems step by step.

What would you do if two customers needed help at the same time?

I would stay calm and speak politely to both customers. I would help one customer first and let the other know that I would be with them soon.

What would you do if you made a mistake?

I would take responsibility for my mistake, inform my manager if necessary, and try to correct it.

Are you comfortable working on weekends?

I understand that retail work can include weekends and busy periods. I am open to discussing the schedule.

What motivates you?

I am motivated by learning new things, meeting people and achieving goals. I also enjoy being part of a team.

How would you describe good customer service?

Good customer service means listening to customers, understanding their needs, being polite, and helping them find the right product.

05 · 10 min · Interview life hacks

Before and during the interview

  1. Dress appropriately — Choose clean, neat, comfortable clothes. Simple smart-casual is a safe option. You do not need to wear a full OYSHO outfit.
  2. Research the brand — Know what OYSHO sells, what makes it different, and why you want to work there. Look at the current collections and the actual store.
  3. Smile and make eye contact — Show that you are friendly and interested. Speak clearly and naturally.
  4. Arrive 10–15 minutes early — Check the location and transport before the interview. Keep your phone on silent.
  5. Ask questions — Prepare at least two questions about the role and team.

What NOT to do

  • Don’t arrive late.
  • Don’t speak negatively about previous employers.
  • Don’t say “I don’t know” and stop there — explain how you would find the answer.
  • Don’t memorise long answers word for word.
  • Don’t interrupt the interviewer.
  • Don’t pretend to have experience you do not have.
  • Don’t use your phone during the interview.

Questions YOU should ask the interviewer

At the end, the interviewer may ask: “Do you have any questions for us?” Prepare two or three.

  • What does a typical working day look like?
  • What training do new employees receive?
  • How big is the team?

  • Ask at least two questions.
  • Recommend a product.
  • Describe two product features.
  • Offer another option.
  • Ask whether the customer wants to try it on.

Extra · Grammar for answers

Build stronger sentences

Use these simple structures to make answers more complete.

  • can: I can work in a team.
  • would like to: I would like to work at OYSHO.
  • because: I want this job because I enjoy helping people.
  • have to: A sales assistant has to be polite.
  • should: We should listen to customers.
  • Past Simple: I worked / I studied / I helped.
Upgrade the answerBasic: “I like this job. I like people.”
Better: “I would like this job because I enjoy meeting people and helping them find products that suit their needs.”
